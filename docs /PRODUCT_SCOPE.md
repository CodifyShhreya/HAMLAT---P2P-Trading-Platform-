  # HAMLAT Product Scope

## Product statement

HAMLAT is a multi-chain P2P marketplace and decentralized exchange experience. This repository implements a non-custodial-looking visual prototype only; it does not connect wallets or execute transactions.

## Primary personas

- Visitor: explores the product and network coverage.
- Marketplace participant: searches and creates sample listings.
- Analyst: reviews illustrative metrics and activity.
- Administrator: future role for moderation and operational controls.

## Prototype acceptance criteria

- Responsive navigation works on desktop and mobile.
- All primary navigation sections render.
- Marketplace search, network filter and side filter work.
- A demo listing can be created and displayed in local state.
- The trading preview clearly states that no order is executed.
- Portfolio and analytics use clearly illustrative values.
- No seed phrase, private key or payment information is requested.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Real-money or token trading
- Wallet signing or custody
- Deposits and withdrawals
- Fiat payment rails
- Escrow or dispute resolution
- Smart contract deployment
- Cross-chain bridges or settlement
- KYC/AML provider integration
- Live market pricing

These capabilities require separate product, security, legal and compliance work.
